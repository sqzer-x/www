Hello! I'm An Hyukjin, operating under the alias SQZER. I am a Software &
Security Engineer and Founder based in South Korea.

I love programming in languages like C++, Rust, and Python. I like being called
'SQZER' because I enjoy squeezing and breaking things down to the bitter end.
Feel free to just call me Squeezer!

## Software & Security Engineer

I enjoy having control over the entire lifecycle of a service—from hardware and
systems to DevSecOps, planning, and development. Because of this, in the offline
world, I have worked in various roles, including Enterprise Infrastructure
Engineer, SysOps Engineer, Full-Stack Developer, and Security Engineer.
Currently, I operate as a Software Engineer within the broader domain of
Security.

Having started my career as a developer, my primary languages are C++, Rust, and
Python. I am also active as an open-source developer, building various tools
required for security testing. I love contributing to other security projects
and tools as well. If you are curious about my work, please check out the
[Projects](@/projects/_index.md) page or my
[GitHub](https://github.com/sqzer-x).

Currently, I have a strong interest in OPSEC and Zero Trust, and I am
continuously dedicating my research and focus to these areas.

## Founder

In my personal time, I dedicate myself to being a Founder. I continuously review
my own services, launch new ones, and pour all my skills and capabilities as a
solo entrepreneur (solopreneur) into exploring new business models, managing
legal responsibilities, and executing marketing strategies.
